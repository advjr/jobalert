Alert feeds

Google Alerts can email or feed you new public posts that match a search, including Facebook group and LinkedIn posts that Google has indexed. Set an alert to deliver to an RSS feed, paste the feed link here, and new posts show up in this tab. Free, no API key.

Your feeds

Accepted: Google Alerts, Talkwalker Alerts, FetchRSS and RSS.app feed links.

How to make one (2 minutes)

  1. Open Google Alerts and paste one of the searches below.
  2. Click Show options, set How often to "As-it-happens", Sources to "Automatic", and Deliver to to "RSS feed". Click Create alert.
  3. Click the RSS icon next to the new alert, copy the page address, and add it above.

Also free: Talkwalker Alerts (same idea, sometimes catches different posts) and FetchRSS, which turns a public Facebook page into a feed. Private Facebook groups can't be read by any of these: use the quick searches for those.
